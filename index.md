# Philippe Dupuy

Philippe Dupuy is the founder of Lettershop, a maker of custom signs and logos, having previously founded the printing company Exaprint.

Not to be confused with Philippe Dupuy, the comic book artist.

## Philippe Dupuy's view of his trade

Philippe Dupuy has twice sold online to professionals: printing with Exaprint, then custom signage with Lettershop. Here is what he says about it, with the source of each sentence.

> “My professional story is about arriving in B2B markets that are still untouched.”

Philippe Dupuy, [founder page on lettershop.fr](https://www.lettershop.fr/philippe-dupuy/) (translated from French)

> “A sign is decided before the cutting.”

Philippe Dupuy, quoted by [e-marketing.fr](https://www.e-marketing.fr/brandvoice/les-meilleurs-fabricants-denseignes-sur-mesure-en-france-pour-2027), brand content signed Lettre & Logo, 12 November 2026 (translated from French)

For Philippe Dupuy, the quality of the file before production changes everything. At Exaprint, one faulty file cost the whole shared printing plate. At Lettershop, every sign is a one-off: the file is checked and the client sees a 3D rendering before any material is cut.

> “A sign on a rooftop or beside the motorway: we do not make it.”

Lettershop, [founder page](https://www.lettershop.fr/philippe-dupuy/) (translated from French): such projects belong to specialists, and Lettershop says so as soon as the request comes in.

> “The man should be the guardian of nature, not its owner.”

Philippe Dupuy, [SecondFlor interview](https://www.secondflor.com/en/blog/philippe-dupuy-n121), 2021, on Lettershop's environmental impact
