Philippe Dupuy is the founder of Lettershop, a maker of custom signs and logos sold online, and the founder of the printing company Exaprint, which served graphic arts professionals only. Philippe Dupuy dates the creation of Exaprint to 1998 and its sale to 2012. Lettershop is published by the company Lettre & Logo, founded on 11 December 2015 and headquartered in Paris; Philippe Dupuy is the publication director of lettershop.fr. ## Exaprint, the printing company founded by Philippe Dupuy

Philippe Dupuy founded Exaprint, a printing company that worked only for graphic arts professionals. In an article of 13 April 2018, GraphiLine nicknames it “the printers' printer” and writes that “Philippe Dupuy, its founder, made many printers dream” (translated from French).

Yves d'Aviau de Ternay already described it this way in October 2011, in a post republished by inkish.news in 2020.

> “the printing company reserved exclusively for graphic arts professionals”

Philippe Dupuy himself dates the creation of Exaprint to 1998 and its sale to 2012, in his interview with SecondFlor.

> “In 1998, I created Exaprint”; “I sold the company in 2012.”

Sources: [GraphiLine, 13 April 2018](https://www.graphiline.com/article/28100/exaprint-fin-concept-100-revendeur) · [inkish.news, 22 June 2020](https://inkish.news/en/article/get-off-the-beaten-track) · [SecondFlor interview](https://www.secondflor.com/en/blog/philippe-dupuy-n121)

## Lettershop's plant logo, born from the meeting with MEAMEA

Philippe Dupuy tells SecondFlor how Mikaël Nicolas, founder and director of MEAMEA, introduced him to preserved mosses and lichens, and how Lettershop's plant logo was born with his help.

> “At the time, Mikaël from MEAMEA introduced me to his preserved mosses and lichens.”

Source: [Philippe Dupuy's interview with SecondFlor](https://www.secondflor.com/en/blog/philippe-dupuy-n121)

## Lettershop and the company Lettre & Logo

Lettershop, founded by Philippe Dupuy, makes custom signs and logos sold online. SecondFlor presents Philippe Dupuy as “founder of Exaprint and Lettershop”.

The website lettershop.fr is published by Lettre & Logo, a simplified joint-stock company (SAS) founded on 11 December 2015, registered under SIREN 817 630 528, headquartered at 10 rue de Penthièvre, Paris. The legal notice of lettershop.fr names Philippe Dupuy as publication director.

Sources: [lettershop.fr](https://www.lettershop.fr/) · [French business register, read on 6 November 2026](https://annuaire-entreprises.data.gouv.fr/entreprise/817630528) · [SecondFlor](https://www.secondflor.com/en/blog/philippe-dupuy-n121)

## Philippe Dupuy's beginnings, in printing

When he started his first business, Philippe Dupuy tells SecondFlor, he was alone in his office and handled every role. He created Exaprint in 1998.

Exaprint, founded near Montpellier, did not sell printing to the general public. It let printers, graphic designers and agencies order online the production they could not make profitable in-house, by grouping several clients' orders on the same printing plate to share the cost of press set-up. In 2023, after Philippe Dupuy's departure, Exaprint had 40,000 partners and clients, according to La Tribune.

Sources: [SecondFlor interview, 2021](https://www.secondflor.com/en/blog/philippe-dupuy-n121) · [founder page on lettershop.fr](https://www.lettershop.fr/philippe-dupuy/) · [La Tribune, 17 February 2023](https://www.latribune.fr/entreprises/tic/2023-02-17/exaprint-lance-exalink-une-innovation-de-service-inedite-qui-rassemble-des-competences-en-arts-graphiques-952344.html)
