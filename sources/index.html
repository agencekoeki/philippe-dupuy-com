What others have published about Philippe Dupuy

Four third-party publications name Philippe Dupuy, and a public register describes the company that publishes Lettershop. For each one, what it states itself is kept apart from what it reports of his words.

GraphiLine · 13 April 2018 · in French

“Exaprint: the end of the 100% reseller concept”

GraphiLine's newsroom names Philippe Dupuy as the founder of Exaprint: “Philippe Dupuy, its founder, made many printers dream.”

Read GraphiLine's article on Exaprint

inkish.news · 22 June 2020

“Get off the beaten track”

Yves d'Aviau de Ternay, in an October 2011 post republished by inkish.news, presents Exaprint as a printing company reserved for graphic arts professionals, and Philippe Dupuy as its founder.

Read Yves d'Aviau de Ternay's post on inkish.news

SecondFlor · interview, 2021

“Meet Philippe Dupuy, founder and CEO of Lettershop”

SecondFlor presents Philippe Dupuy as the founder of Exaprint and Lettershop. In the interview, Philippe Dupuy tells of creating Exaprint in 1998, selling it in 2012, and the birth of the plant logo with Mikaël Nicolas of MEAMEA: these dates and this story are his own words.

Read the interview published by SecondFlor

e-marketing.fr · 12 November 2026 · brand content, in French

“The best custom sign makers in France for 2027”

An article in the Brandvoice section, signed Lettre & Logo and produced in partnership with Lettershop: it is brand content, not newsroom journalism. It quotes Philippe Dupuy, founder of Lettershop: “A sign is decided before the cutting.”

Read the article on e-marketing.fr

La Tribune · 17 February 2023 · in French

“Exaprint launches Exalink”

La Tribune writes that Exaprint has 40,000 partners and clients in 2023. The article is about Exaprint after Philippe Dupuy's departure and does not name him.

Read La Tribune's article on Exaprint
